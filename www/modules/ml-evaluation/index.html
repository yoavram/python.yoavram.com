Machine Learning Evaluation

For teams working with ML models.

What you will be able to do

  • Design held-out evaluation that matches how the model will be used.
  • Find and diagnose leakage and selection bias.
  • Compare models honestly, and report how much to trust the comparison.

Prerequisites

Python experience, including NumPy and pandas, and some prior exposure to machine learning.

Format and duration

1–2 days, on-site or remote. Hands-on, with exercises.

Outline

Three half-days. TODO(Yoav): what should this workshop cover now that Bayesian inference, calibration and imbalanced data have moved to other workshops?

  1. Held-out evaluation: splits, metrics and what each tells you
  2. Leakage and selection bias: how they arise and how to find them
  3. Honest comparison of models

Machine Learning Evaluation and Uncertainty

For teams working with ML models.

What you will be able to do

  • Design held-out evaluation that matches how the model will be used.
  • Find and diagnose leakage and selection bias.
  • Handle imbalanced data, including with augmentation.
  • Build and evaluate time-series models, and compare models honestly.
  • Fit models by likelihood and by Bayesian inference, run MCMC and check that it worked.
  • Check whether predicted probabilities are calibrated, and attach conformal prediction intervals to a model's predictions.

Prerequisites

Python experience, including NumPy and pandas, some prior exposure to machine learning, and a basic understanding of probability and statistics.

Format and duration

2–3 days, on-site or remote. Hands-on, with exercises.

Three-day version, six half-days. The two-day version drops the time-series and Bayesian regression material. TODO(Yoav): check, since the Bayesian topics were two half-days before and are one here

  2. Leakage and selection bias: how they arise and how to find them

  4. Time-series models, and honest comparison of models
  5. Likelihood versus Bayesian inference: MCMC and Bayesian regression
  6. Calibration and conformal prediction

Can be tailored to

Your team's models, data and deployment setting, and to a shorter version.

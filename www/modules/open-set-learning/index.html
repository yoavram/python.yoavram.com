Open-Set Learning: Metric Learning, Zero-Shot and Re-Identification

For teams whose models must handle classes, individuals or items they have not seen.

What you will be able to do

  • Build a Siamese network and explain what it learns.
  • Explain the difference between closed-set and open-set classification, and evaluate a model on classes it never saw in training.
  • Classify zero-shot with CLIP, and measure what prompt wording changes.
  • Train an embedding with metric learning and score the resulting metric space.
  • Build a re-identification pipeline: split data honestly, match queries against a database, reject individuals who are not in it, and cluster.

Prerequisites

Experience training a neural network, for example from Deep Learning from First Principles.

The worked examples use images; the same ideas apply wherever you can learn an embedding.

Format and duration

2 days, on-site or remote. Hands-on, with exercises.

Outline

  1. Siamese networks and open sets: learning a similarity, open-set classification, and evaluating on unseen classes
  2. Zero-shot classification with CLIP, and metric learning: training and scoring an embedding
  3. Re-identification: honest splits, and matching queries against a database
  4. Re-identification, continued: rejecting unknown individuals, and clustering

Can be tailored to

Your team's data and your own definition of "same" and "new".
